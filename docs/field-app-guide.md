# Representative's Guide: the Field CRM App

The app runs in the phone's browser, so there is nothing to download from Google Play or the App Store. You add it to your home screen once. After that it opens like any other app, and it also works without an internet connection.

---

## 1. First time: install and sign in

Your company will send you the link, your user name and your password.

### On Android (Chrome)
1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three dots **⋮** at the top right, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open the app from the new icon on your home screen, not from the browser.

### On iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the link in **Safari**. It must be Safari, not Chrome.
2. Tap the Share button **⬆️** at the bottom, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
3. Open the app from the icon on your home screen. On iPhone, notifications only work when the app is opened from that icon.

### Signing in
- Enter your email and password, then tap **Sign In**.
- Your first sign-in needs an internet connection, so the app can download your plan, your customers and the products. After that you can work offline.

---

## 2. Permissions: allow all of them

| Permission | Why it is needed | What to choose |
|---|---|---|
| **Location** | To check in to visits | **Allow while using the app**, and turn on **Precise location** |
| **Camera** | Receipt photos for collections and expenses | **Allow** |
| **Notifications** | Order and expense approvals or rejections, and reminders | **Allow** |

### Settings on the phone itself
- **Date and time:** keep them on **Automatic**. If you set the clock by hand, your visits are flagged to your manager as "clock suspect".
- **Location:** turn on **High accuracy** (Google Location Accuracy) in the location settings.
- **Battery saver:** it makes GPS slower and less accurate. Turn it off during working hours, or exclude Chrome or Safari from it.

If you refused a permission by mistake, open the site settings in your browser and set it back to **Allow**.

---

## 3. Fingerprint sign-in

You don't have to type your password every time:

1. On the home screen, tap **Set up** on the **Sign in with your fingerprint** card. You can also open **Security** at the top.
2. Confirm with your fingerprint or face.
3. From then on, tap **Sign in with fingerprint** on the sign-in screen.

Notes:
- Fingerprint sign-in is tied to this phone only. If you change phones, sign in with your password and set it up again.
- If you lose your phone, tell your company straight away so they can block sign-in from it.

## 4. Your working day

### Today's plan
- Tap **Plan** at the bottom. Today's visits are shown in order, on a map and in a list.
- The visit that is up next has a highlighted **Start visit** button.
- To visit someone who isn't in your plan, tap **Unplanned visit** on the home screen.

### A visit, step by step
1. Tap **Start visit** on the doctor or pharmacy.
2. While you are **actually at the customer**, tap **Get my location & check in** and wait for the location. It usually takes a few seconds.
3. The location result appears:
   - **Location matched:** all good, tap **Check in now**.
   - **Location uncertain**, or the app says you are away from the customer: tap **Try location again** near the door or a window. If you are still away, choose the real reason from the list and continue.
   - The customer has no saved location yet: tick **Save my position as this customer's location** so it is saved.
4. Record the visit: the products you discussed, the samples you left, and your notes. At a pharmacy you can also tap **Create order**.
5. When you have finished, tap **Complete visit**, then **Check out & save visit** while you are still at the customer.

### Tips for an accurate location
- Check in at the entrance or near a window, not in a basement or deep inside a mall.
- If your location is far off, **don't make up a reason**. Choose the real one. Your manager sees the reasons, and the right reason helps us correct the customer's saved location.
- If GPS won't work at all, choose **Check in without location** and give the reason. The visit is still recorded, but it is flagged for review.
- You can't have two visits open at the same time. Finish the previous visit first, or tap **Cancel visit** on it.

### Orders, collections, returns and expenses

| What you want to do | Where |
|---|---|
| Order | During the visit, tap **Create order**. Or go to **Orders**, then **+ New order**. You can tap **Save draft** and finish it later. When it is ready, tap **Submit for approval** |
| Collection | On the home screen, tap **Collection**. Or go to **Custody**, then **+ Collection**. Take a photo of the receipt |
| Return | On the home screen, tap **Return** |
| Expense | On the home screen, tap **Expense**. Take a photo of the invoice |
| Custody | Tap **Custody** at the bottom: the stock and cash you are holding |

When your manager approves or rejects something, you get a notification, and you will also find it under **Alerts**.

## 5. Working offline

- When the connection drops, you see **You're offline — showing saved data** at the top. Carry on as normal: visits, orders, collections and expenses all work.
- Everything you do is saved on the phone and sent automatically when the connection returns.
- The sync indicator at the top shows how many items are waiting to be sent. Tap it to open **Pending sync**:
  - **Sync now:** send everything now.
  - **Try again:** retry an item that didn't go through.
  - **Discard:** only if your manager tells you to. Anything you discard can't be recovered.

### Never do these
- **Don't tap Sign out** while items are still waiting to be sent. The app warns you, because those items would be lost.
- **Don't clear your browser data** (Clear data or Clear cache) for Chrome or Safari.
- **Don't remove the app** from your home screen while items are still waiting to be sent.

---

## 6. Updates

- When a new version is available, a bar with an **Update** button appears.
- Tap it once you have finished what you are doing. The app never updates by itself while you are entering an order.
- Items waiting to be sent are not lost during an update.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Location is slow or inaccurate | On the home screen, tap **GPS check** and step outside the building. Make sure precise location is on and battery saver is off |
| "Notifications are blocked" | Set notifications back to **Allow** in your browser's site settings |
| Notifications don't arrive on iPhone | Open the app from the home screen icon, not from Safari. Then tap **Alerts**, then **Turn on notifications** |
| Fingerprint sign-in doesn't work | Sign in with your password, and set it up again from **Security** |
| An item has been waiting to send for a long time | Open **Pending sync** and read the message next to it. If you don't understand it, take a screenshot and send it to your manager |
| Forgot your password | Contact your company |

---

## 8. Your privacy

- Your location is recorded **only while the app is open during working hours** (8 am to 8 pm), and at every check-in and check-out.
- Outside working hours the app does not track you. The one exception is when you tap **Start route** in **Plan** to record your route yourself: tracking then stays on until you tap **Stop route**.
- The photos you take (receipts and invoices) go only to your company account.
